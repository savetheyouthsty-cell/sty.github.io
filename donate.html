Where Your Money Goes

Save the Youth is run entirely by student volunteers, so we have no salaries or overhead. Donations pay for program materials, translation and printing costs, outreach to partner schools, website and software fees, and the tools we use to match and track student pairs.

Other Ways to Give

Many employers match charitable donations made by their employees. If your company offers matching gifts, you can often double your contribution at no extra cost. We also welcome in-kind support such as printing, design work, and translation help. Reach out through our contact page to discuss.

Questions About Donating

If you would like documentation of our nonprofit status, a receipt for a past gift, or information about supporting a specific program, email us at savetheyouth.sty@gmail.com and we will respond within 48 hours.
